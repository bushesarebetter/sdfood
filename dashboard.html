Inspect smarter: ordering San Diego's routine food inspections by each facility's record

By Ayan Pendharkar. Independent student project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the City of San Diego or the County of San Diego. The County says its inspection methodology "prioritizes inspections based on relative risk". In its published record, how often a place is inspected depends mostly on what kind of place it is, and the order a month's inspections are done in shows no risk ordering. Within a month's schedule, this orders the routine inspections by each facility's own record.
