Inspect smarter: ordering San Diego's routine food inspections by each facility's record

Independent analysis by Ayan Pendharkar, not affiliated with or endorsed by the County of San Diego · The County sets routine inspection frequency by facility category, and follow-up visits respond to findings. Within a month's schedule, this orders the routine inspections by each facility's own record.
