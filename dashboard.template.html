Inspect smarter: risk-targeting for San Diego food inspections

Independent analysis by Ayan Pendharkar · San Diego County DEH runs ~21,000 routine inspections/yr on a fixed calendar — this ranks them by risk instead.

The evidence (forward-tested, out-of-sample)

Inspect the top 20% by risk → find ~45% of all critical violations (vs 20% by calendar). Tested on 36,543 inspections the model never saw.

Critical violations found ~6 days earlier within the monthly cycle (95% CI 5.8–6.5), 18 days within a quarter — while clean facilities wait under a day longer.

Statistically checked: model AUC is stable at 0.73–0.75 across three separate time cutoffs (rolling-origin backtest); every figure is from data after the training window; the days-earlier estimate has a bootstrap 95% CI. Full method & code in the repo.
